Frequently Asked Questions

🕐 What time is delivery?

Tiffins are delivered between 12:30 PM and 1:30 PM daily. Let us know your preferred time during signup.

💰 How is payment handled?

We'll send you an invoice at the end of each month. Pay via bank transfer or UPI. No hidden charges.

⏸️ Can I pause my subscription?

Yes! Pause for weeks or months anytime. For daily orders, message us before 8 PM the previous evening.

🍛 What if I'm vegetarian?

All menus are vegetarian. Non-veg variations available on request — let us know during signup.

🚫 What about weekends & holidays?

We observe major holidays. Notify us in advance if you need special arrangements.

❓ How do I manage add-ons?

After signing up, you'll have a dashboard. Choose chapatis or rice for each day from there.
